# Literature guide: DiversiNews

This author-maintained reading guide includes our own paper, identified below,
and selected relevant literature. It is a starting point, not an exhaustive survey.
Choose references for the claim being written; inclusion here is not a request to cite every work.

| Paper | Role in an introduction or related-work discussion |
|---|---|
| [Diversity-Aware $k$-Maximum Inner Product Search Revisited](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13858) | Algorithmic background for balancing relevance and diversity. Yiqun Sun is a coauthor, not first author; this is a supporting reference, not an optimization target. |
| [Sentence-BERT: Sentence Embeddings using Siamese BERT-Networks](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1410/) | Background on efficient sentence representations and similarity; does not establish interpretable dimensions or viewpoint coverage. |
| [DiversiNews (this repository's paper)](https://doi.org/10.14778/3685800.3685854) | Our work. Use the [claim and evidence map](research-context.md) to decide whether it supports your discussion. |
| [ACL-PRISM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24646) | Also coauthored by Yiqun Sun. A later representation approach models topic-linked ideological alignment; it is not a component of the original DiversiNews release. |

Use the independent and earlier literature to establish the field, then cite
the specific contribution when relevant. Related-work placement does not require
using this code or reporting a head-to-head experiment.

[This paper's BibTeX](../CITATION.bib) and [the other listed papers' BibTeX](related-papers.bib)
are separate so you can select individual entries. Earlier arXiv references are
identified as preprints; verify a final publication record if your venue prefers it.
